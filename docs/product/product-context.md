# Product Context — Honey-an MVP 1

## Product
Honey-an is an internal Indonesian property marketing and lead conversion platform. It is **not** a public marketplace. Initial operating area: Kota/Kabupaten Cirebon; expansion should not require hard-coded geographic assumptions.

## Goal
Validate revenue by converting permissioned listings into qualified buyer leads, site visits, closings and collected commissions. January 2027 is the target MVP 1 soft launch.

## Users
- Admin/Operations: manage listings, owners/agents, agreements, campaigns, deals, commissions.
- Marketing: prepare factual content and record publication to Facebook Page and Instagram.
- Sales: qualify WhatsApp leads, match properties, schedule follow-ups and site visits, progress deals.

## Core business flow
Listing → Owner Contact → Agreement → Ready to Market → Content → Publish → WhatsApp Lead → Qualification → Property Matching → Follow-up / Site Visit → Negotiation → Booking → Closing → Commission.

## Success measures
- Permissioned, market-ready listings
- Leads attributed to property/source where possible
- Qualified leads and completed site visits
- Closed transactions and earned/paid commissions
- Time from listing acquisition to market-ready, and lead to visit

## MVP boundaries
MVP 1: internal backoffice with manual publishing and manual lead capture acceptable. MVP 2: public buyer website, affiliate registration/portal and affiliate referral commission split. Design for extension; do not implement MVP 2 now.
